# Model card: EPA sector-flow benchmark

## Purpose

Explore whether broad sector classifications and waste-management pathway codes can explain variation in EPA's modeled 2018 national food-waste flow amounts. This is a portfolio benchmark and an entry point for sector-level investigation.

## Data and target

The benchmark uses the EPA Flow-By-Sector method 2 CSV. Each row represents a modeled national flow, with `FlowAmount` in kilograms. The target is `log1p(FlowAmount)`. The source values are estimates generated by EPA's FLOWSA modeling process; they are not direct measurements of retail surplus or individual donation events.

## Model and evaluation

Ridge regression over one-hot NAICS producer-sector prefixes and consuming-sector codes. Hyperparameter selection uses a validation split. The final test set holds out complete producing-sector codes, so performance measures transfer to unseen producer codes. The benchmark compares against a median-only baseline and reports MAE, RMSE, and R-squared on the log-transformed target.

## Intended use

Demonstrate data ingestion, feature engineering, grouped validation, baseline comparison, and model limitations. Results may motivate further sector research; the model is not used by the matching UI.

## Limitations

The data cover one modeled year and are aggregated at the sector-flow level. It cannot predict current local surplus, donation acceptance, food safety, or recipient demand. A grouped split tests transfer across sector codes but does not provide temporal validation. Stronger decision support requires partner-level event data, time-aware validation, and domain review.

## Human oversight

The web app uses transparent eligibility rules and a deterministic ranking score. People verify food handling and pickup. The regression benchmark never approves or routes donations.
